📄 Math Cheat Sheets

Quick formulas and free printable sheets.

Common Formulas

  • Area of a rectangle: length × width
  • Perimeter of a rectangle: 2 × (length + width)
  • Area of a triangle: ½ × base × height
  • Volume of a box: length × width × height
  • Average (mean): add all numbers, then divide by how many

🤖 What Is AI?

AI is a computer program that learns from examples. It can recognize things or answer questions, but it is not alive and can make mistakes. Always check what it says!

⚛️ What Is Quantum Computing?

Normal computers use bits that are 0 or 1. Quantum computers use qubits that can be both at the same time — like a coin spinning in the air.

🛡️ Kids Online Safety

Simple safety tips for kids and parents.

  • Stay with an adult. Kids should use the internet with a parent nearby.
  • Never share personal information. Do not type your real name, school, or address online.
  • Use the free versions only. No accounts or payments needed to start.
  • Check what you see. If something feels wrong, stop and ask a parent.
  • Be kind online. Treat others the way you want to be treated.

Helpful resources:

  • Common Sense Media
  • FTC: Protecting Your Child’s Privacy

🚀 How to Start Today

Simple tips for parents of a 2nd or 5th grader.

  • Pick one thing. Choose Scratch or Khan Academy and spend 15–20 minutes together.
  • Sit with them the first time. Kids do better when a parent is nearby.
  • Celebrate small wins. Finished something? High-five and stop.
  • Keep it free. All links have free versions. No accounts required to start.
